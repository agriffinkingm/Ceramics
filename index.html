Fingerstrument

Hold both hands up to your camera. Point a finger inside the left wheel to pick a key and inside the right wheel to pick a chord. The chord sustains while both hands are visible. Click the video to hear the current chord. On iPhone, flip the ring/silent switch off and turn the volume up to hear sound.
